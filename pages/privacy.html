Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 October 2026

1. Overview

Schedule Bridge (“the Application”) is a personal tool intended to synchronize a university timetable with the user's Google Calendar. This policy explains what information the Application may process and how it is handled.

2. Information processed

Timetable information may include course names, dates, times, locations, groups, and instructor details where present. Google Calendar data is processed as needed to create or update events. OAuth access tokens and refresh tokens may be stored to maintain authorization. Limited diagnostic information may be written to logs to troubleshoot errors.

3. How information is used

Information is used to authenticate the user, process timetable information, synchronize relevant events with Google Calendar, and diagnose failures. The Application does not sell personal information or use Google user data for advertising.

4. Storage and sharing

The Application is designed for personal use. OAuth credentials may be stored locally on the device running the Application. Other people with access to that device or its files may be able to access locally stored data. Information is not intentionally sold or shared, except as needed to provide the requested functionality through Google APIs or the timetable source, as required by law, or as directed by the user.

5. Google API Services

The Application's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs complies with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements. Google data is used only to provide the features described in this policy, is not used for advertising, and is not sold to data brokers.

6. Retention and deletion

Data may remain in local application files, logs, or Google Calendar until deleted by the user. The user can stop future access by revoking the Application's access in Google Account settings and can remove locally stored credentials by deleting the token storage. Calendar events already created may need to be deleted separately.

7. Security

Use appropriate protections for the device and locally stored credentials. No storage or transmission method is completely secure. Do not publish credentials, OAuth client secrets, access tokens, or refresh tokens in source control or public logs.

8. Children's privacy

The Application is not designed specifically for children and should not be used by children without appropriate authorization and supervision.

9. Changes

This policy may be updated if the Application's functionality or data practices change. The revised version will be posted on this page with an updated date.
